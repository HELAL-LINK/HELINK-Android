# What's new in HELINK 1.6.0 (one line each)
Download a whole YouTube course or playlist at once: choose the quality once, and the videos are saved numbered in order, in a folder named after the course.
HELINK marks the videos you downloaded before, so you don't download them twice.
HELINK tells you about new versions when you're online, and updates itself when you say so, with a "Not now" button.
