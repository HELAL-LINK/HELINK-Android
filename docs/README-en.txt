HELINK: video downloader (Android 11 and newer) - version 1.7.1
==============================================================
Downloads videos from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X (Twitter) in the quality you
choose, or as MP3 audio, downloads a whole YouTube course or playlist at once, or just a part of any
video, and saves the WhatsApp statuses you viewed. It can also take
the music out of videos and audio, keeping only the speech or singing: while downloading, from
finished downloads, or from any audio or video file on the phone. The app works in Arabic and
English.

New in version 1.7.1
--------------------
- Download just a part of any video: play it, set where it starts and ends on the timeline, then
  save it as video or MP3 audio (see "Downloading a part of a video").
- Save the WhatsApp (and WhatsApp Business) statuses you viewed, photos and videos, before they
  disappear after 24 hours (see "WhatsApp statuses").
- Four sections at the top: Download, Clip, Statuses, Downloads.

New in version 1.6.0
--------------------
- Download a whole YouTube course or playlist at once: choose the quality once, and the videos are
  saved numbered in order, in a folder named after the course (see "Downloading a whole course or
  playlist").
- The app marks the videos you downloaded before, so you don't download them twice.
- The app tells you about new versions when you're online, and updates itself when you say so,
  with a "Not now" button (see "Updating the app").

Installing (once)
-----------------
1) Open the app file (HELINK-1.7.1.apk) from WhatsApp, the Files app, or Downloads.
   It can also be downloaded from HELINK's page: github.com/HELAL-LINK/HELINK-Android
2) If you see that installing from this source isn't allowed:
   tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", then go back and tap Install.
3) If Google Play Protect says it doesn't know the developer:
   tap "More details", then "Install anyway". This appears for any new app from outside the store.
4) Open HELINK. The first time only, it downloads its tools (about 50 MB) and sets itself up in a
   minute or two. Keep the internet on and the app open until it finishes. If the internet drops,
   tap Try again to continue where it stopped.

If you have an earlier version (ALI HELAL or HELINK), install the new one over it; there's no
need to remove the old one, and your downloads and settings stay as they are.
Once 1.6.0 is installed you won't need these steps again: the app tells you about every new version
itself and updates when you say so (see "Updating the app").

Changing the language
---------------------
Tap the language button at the top of the screen ("EN" for English, "عربي" for Arabic). Every
screen switches at once, including running downloads and notifications, and the app remembers it.

Downloading
-----------
- Fastest way: in YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or X, tap Share and pick HELINK. The app
  opens, finds the link even if text came with it, and gets the available qualities: all that's
  left is to tap Download.
- Or: tap Share > Copy link, open HELINK and tap Paste.
  (The app never reads what you copied on its own, only when you tap Paste.)
- Choose Video or MP3 audio, pick a quality (720p is marked "Best pick"), and tap Download.
- The app takes you to Downloads; you can go back to Download and add another video right away.

Downloading a whole course or playlist (new in 1.6.0)
-----------------------------------------------------
1) In YouTube, open the playlist (the course), tap Share and pick HELINK, or copy its link and
   paste it in HELINK. If you open a video that's part of a playlist, a card "This video is part of
   a playlist" appears under it, with "Download the whole list".
2) The app reads the list and shows its name, channel, number of videos and total length.
3) Choose Video or MP3 audio, then the quality once for every video: "Up to 720p" (Best pick) means
   each video comes at 720p, or at the best it has if that's lower. You can turn on "Remove the
   music" too.
4) Every video is selected; untick the ones you don't want (or "Select all" again). Private or
   removed videos are greyed out as "Not available" and aren't downloaded.
5) Tap "Download 13 videos" (the estimated size is under it). They're all added to Downloads and
   download in order, two at a time; each shows the course's name.

- They're saved in a folder named after the course: "Download" > "HELINK" > "<course name>", each
  numbered in the list's order ("01 - Welcome", "02 - ..."), so the lessons stay in order in any
  app.
- Videos you downloaded before (or that are downloading now) show "In downloads" and aren't
  selected, so nothing comes twice. You can select them yourself to download them again.
- For very big lists, the screen shows the first 1000 videos.
- A whole channel isn't downloaded at once: open the playlist you want on the channel and copy its
  link.
- The "Mix" lists YouTube makes by itself aren't courses, so only the video you opened is
  downloaded from them.
- Whole lists can be downloaded from YouTube. From other sites, copy the video's own link.

Downloading a part of a video (new in 1.7.1)
--------------------------------------------
To download one minute of a long lecture, a piece of a song, or a moment of a match, from any site
the app works with.
1) Open "Clip" at the top and paste the video's link. Or, on the quality screen of "Download",
   tap "Download only a part of the video".
2) The video shows, with the timeline under it and two orange markers on it: the start and the
   end. The part is what lies between them.
3) Tap the video to play it. When you reach the moment you want, tap "Start here", then keep
   watching and tap "End here". You can also drag the markers, or tap "−" and "+" to move each one
   by a second.
4) Tap "Play the part" to watch the part you chose from start to end; it stops at its end.
5) Choose Video or MP3 audio and the quality (the size shown is for the part alone), then
   "Download the part".
- Only the part is downloaded, not the whole video, and it is cut exactly at the second you chose.
- It shows in Downloads with its start and end times, and is saved in "Download" > "HELINK" with
  the video's name and the part's times (like "clip 1.20-2.05").
- Some sites don't let the video play inside the app: a note says so on the video, and you can
  still choose the part by its times and download it.

WhatsApp statuses (new in 1.7.1)
--------------------------------
WhatsApp keeps the statuses you viewed for 24 hours, and HELINK can save them before they go.
1) Open "Statuses" and tap "Allow reading statuses".
2) Your phone opens the statuses folder right away: tap "Use this folder", then "Allow". This
   happens only once. (If you have WhatsApp Business too, you can allow it the same way.)
3) The statuses you viewed show, newest first. Tap one to see it large and move between them, then
   Save. Or tap "Save all" at the bottom.
- They're saved in "Download" > "HELINK" > "WhatsApp" and show in the Gallery. Saved statuses are
  marked "Saved".
- Only statuses you viewed in WhatsApp show. If one is missing, open it in WhatsApp first, then
  come back and tap Refresh.
- If you see "That's not the statuses folder", tap Allow again and don't change the folder your
  phone opens: tap "Use this folder" as it is.
- Statuses belong to the people who posted them: don't share them without their permission.

Removing the music (professional in 1.5.0)
------------------------------------------
Music removal on the phone now uses the same professional remover as HELINK for Windows (the AI
model Kim_Vocal_2). It runs on the phone itself, with no internet. It takes the music out very
precisely and keeps speech and singing clear, at the sound's full quality.

Three ways to use it:
1) While downloading: under the qualities there's a "Remove the music" switch, for both Video and
   MP3 audio. Turn it on, then tap Download (it says "No music" under it). For a video, the picture
   stays exactly as it is (copied, not encoded again); only the music is taken out of its sound.
2) From finished downloads: under any finished video or audio there's "Remove the music from this
   file". A new copy without music is made; the original stays as it is.
3) From any file on the phone: on the main screen, the card "Remove the music from a file on your
   phone" > "Choose a file" (you can pick several). Or from any app (Files, Gallery, WhatsApp...):
   Share the audio or video file and pick "Remove music (HELINK)". The original isn't changed.

The new copy is saved in "Download" > "HELINK" with "(no music)" at the end of its name ("(بدون
موسيقى)" in Arabic), in the same kind of file as the original:
- MP3 stays MP3 at the same quality, with its cover picture, artist and other tags.
- M4A, OGG, OPUS, FLAC and WAV keep their kind; rare kinds (such as WMA) become MP3.
- Videos (MP4, MKV, WEBM, MOV) keep their picture, subtitles and chapters as they are.

What the file's card shows, in order:
- The first time only, the app downloads the music remover (about 67 MB) and prepares it; this
  won't happen again: "Downloading the music remover", then "Preparing the music remover".
- "Preparing the file" (files of the phone): the file is copied into the app first.
- "Removing the music" with the percentage and the time left. A song takes a few minutes depending
  on the phone; long lectures take longer. You can leave the app and use your phone; the
  percentage shows in the notifications.
- Then "Finishing up" (for a video, "Joining sound and picture"), and it's saved in Downloads.

Notes:
- Pause during the removal, then Resume, continues where it stopped, not from the start, even if
  the app was closed or the phone restarted in between. The file isn't downloaded again.
- Music is removed from one file at a time; the next one shows "Waiting to remove the music".
  Other downloads keep going meanwhile.
- The removal runs at low priority so the phone stays smooth, but it uses the whole processor and
  the phone may get a little warm; for long files, it's best to keep the phone on the charger.
- It needs about 450 MB of the phone's memory, and uses less on its own on phones with less
  memory. If you see "Not enough memory", close other open apps, then tap Try again.
- The app remembers the "Remove the music" switch for next time.
- To be clear: the removal uses AI. It takes the music out very clearly (beats, drums, piano,
  guitar, violins and so on), but a very faint trace can remain in some difficult clips.
- The old remover that version 1.4.0 used is deleted on its own (frees 39 MB).

Updating the app (new in 1.6.0)
-------------------------------
- When you're online, HELINK looks for a new version a little after it opens, then every few
  hours. It never downloads or installs anything by itself.
- When there's a new version, a window says "A new version of HELINK is available", with the
  version, its size and what's new, and two buttons:
  Update now: downloads the update (about 6 MB), checks that it really is HELINK's, then the phone
     asks you to confirm the install: tap Update (or Install). The app closes for a moment, then a
     "HELINK was updated" notification appears: tap it to open the new version.
  Not now: nothing changes, and the app reminds you a day later. Meanwhile an Update button stays at
     the top of the screen, so you can update whenever you like.
- On the first update only, Android asks you to let HELINK install updates: tap Open settings, turn
  on "Allow from this source", then come back to HELINK; it finishes the update by itself.
- Your downloads, settings and files stay as they are, and downloads that were running continue
  after the update.
- If the internet drops while the update downloads, tap Try again to continue where it stopped.
- To check yourself: the (i) button > Updates > Check for updates.
- Updates only come from HELINK's page (github.com/HELAL-LINK/HELINK-Android), and each one is
  signed: the app turns down any file that isn't HELINK's, and Android refuses any copy not signed
  with HELINK's key.

Downloads: pause, resume and delete
-----------------------------------
At the top there are two sections: Download and Downloads. In Downloads each file has a progress
bar, a percentage (for example 45%) and its status:
- "Downloading": in progress. Two files download at once; the others wait as "Queued" and start
  on their own when a running download finishes.
- "Paused - waiting to resume": the part already downloaded is kept on the phone.
Each download has these buttons:
- Pause: stops the download and keeps what was downloaded.
- Resume: continues from the last byte, not from the beginning.
- Delete: cancels the download and deletes the partial file to free space (tap twice to confirm).
If the app is closed, the phone turns off or the internet drops, the download stays in the list at
the percentage it reached; tap Resume or Try again to finish it.
- You can leave the app and use your phone as usual: downloads keep going on their own and show in
  the notifications, where you can also pause them all.
- Finished downloads appear under "Completed": Play opens the file in your phone's video or music
  app, Share sends it to WhatsApp, for example, and "Remove the music from this file" makes a copy
  without music. Removing it from the list doesn't delete it.

Where are the files saved?
--------------------------
In the "Download" > "HELINK" folder on the phone. They also appear in the Gallery and in music apps.
Each course's videos are in a folder named after it inside "HELINK", and WhatsApp statuses in
"WhatsApp" inside it. (Files downloaded with the
very first version stay in "Download" > "ALI HELAL".)

If a post has several videos (common on X and Instagram), small numbered pictures appear: pick the
video you want.

About
-----
The (i) button at the top opens About: the version and updates, the app and its features, its
owner, and contact details (call or WhatsApp).

Good to know
------------
- The app downloads public videos that anyone can watch without signing in. Private accounts and
  videos that need a sign-in can't be downloaded.
- YouTube takes a little longer than other sites while "Getting the video's details" (a few seconds
  to half a minute).
- Instagram sometimes refuses after many requests in a row: wait 15 minutes and try again.
- TikTok photo posts have no video, so the app downloads their music as MP3.
- The app updates its download tool by itself every few days (about 3 MB) to keep working with the
  sites.
- The app file is about 6 MB and takes about 200 MB on the phone after setup, plus paused
  downloads (their partial files are kept until you finish or delete them), plus 33 MB for the
  music remover if you use it. While it removes the music from a file, the app needs some room for
  a while (about 21 MB per minute of sound), deleted when it's done.
- No ads, and no built-in player: files open in your phone's own apps.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- Try again usually fixes it: the app updates itself when a site changes, and a download that lost
  the internet continues where it stopped.
- A Samsung phone refuses to install the app at all? Auto Blocker may be on: Settings > Security and
  privacy > Auto Blocker; turn it off, install the app, then turn it back on.
- No download notification? Allow notifications for HELINK in the app's settings.
- If a download didn't finish, tap "Copy details for support" under its message and send what was
  copied.
- The update didn't finish, or the phone says "App not installed"? Tap Try again, or download the new
  version from HELINK's page (the link is in the update window) and install it over the old one.
- HELINK isn't in the list when you share an audio or video file? Look for "Remove music (HELINK)"
  in the share list, or use "Choose a file" inside the app.

Uninstalling
------------
Like any app: long-press the HELINK icon, then Uninstall.
Videos you downloaded stay in "Download" > "HELINK".
